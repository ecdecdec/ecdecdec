Meiirkhan Yermek, Petropavlovsk, Kazakhstan. High school student (NIS, class of 2027).

Since 2022 I've run a small marketing agency for local businesses, and most of what I build
started as a tool for that. Lately I'm more interested in how LLM agents make decisions when
the data is uncertain, and what to do when they disagree.

Projects:

- [swarmer](https://github.com/ecdecdec/swarmer) — a group of LLM agents votes on a value,
  outliers are filtered, the consensus is signed and checked by a Solana program before it
  changes on-chain state. Rust/Anchor, TypeScript.
- [missio](https://github.com/ecdecdec/missio) — helps school students in Kazakhstan find grants,
  exchanges and olympiads they qualify for and track deadlines. Next.js, Supabase.
- sales-agent — finds businesses on 2GIS, scores how much they need marketing, and contacts them
  over WhatsApp/Telegram. I used it to find clients for the agency. TypeScript, SQLite.
